## 3. Defined, disabled and ambiguous alternatives

Omega supplies substantive Hyprland Lua and Zsh/Starship settings. Its Neovim native configuration is an out-of-store checkout prerequisite. A user-shell zsh request alone is not treated as a verified login change for the custom systemManager class; the custom account policy defaults account management off ([`modules/policies/define-user.nix:7`](https://github.com/Le0-dot/config.nix/blob/4171c4b9f1f2ad0dbcab61f829cea31135064057/modules/policies/define-user.nix#L7)). No Emacs adoption follows from Zsh emacs keymap.
