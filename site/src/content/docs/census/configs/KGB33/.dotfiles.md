## Defined, disabled and ambiguous alternatives

The misleadingly named nushell-login actually selects Bash ([modules/aspects/apps/nushell.nix:16](https://github.com/KGB33/.dotfiles/blob/851fb5b43c9e57a1fc5dfff6169b33b960a40932/modules/aspects/apps/nushell.nix#L16)) and conditionally execs Nu from its initializer ([modules/aspects/apps/nushell.nix:22](https://github.com/KGB33/.dotfiles/blob/851fb5b43c9e57a1fc5dfff6169b33b960a40932/modules/aspects/apps/nushell.nix#L22)). Darwin Zsh likewise conditionally execs Nu; that does not prove an account default. Starship presets and tmux bindings are substantive. Portal strings named gnome and emacs keybinding modes are not GNOME or Emacs adoption.

## Machine roles and remaining unknowns

No physical laptop/desktop/server role is established for the three hosts from application selection alone. The Darwin target is a standalone home, not a managed Darwin host. The Linux standalone home shares the identity’s HM definitions; its host-dependent setHost aspect and OS contributions do not establish a separate physical machine or prove successful evaluation.
