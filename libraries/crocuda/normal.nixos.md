## Reading by Reader A

### Shape summary
Namespace `normal`: the same author's desktop config (Firefox with arkenfox, Niri + Waybar + Mudras, Neovim/NvChad, Kitty, Git/jj, SearxNG, Yubikey, gaming, printing). Aspect leaves live under `modules/aspects/`; native dotfiles sit beside them. Two constructors (`fontSize`, `screenSize`) and two typed options give the only real parametrization. Structure and flake plumbing are copied from crocuda.nixos, defects included. The README opens with "These are my public dots. I do not recommend installing" ([`README.md:10-14`](https://github.com/crocuda/normal.nixos/blob/c8b5cf80b70ec9f052640518c09dedaa6e8c3546/README.md#L10-L14)).
